Porcelain veneers

A veneer is a thin porcelain shell bonded to the front of a tooth. Veneers can change the shape, colour and length of teeth for a more even smile.

{{book_buttons}}

What veneers can help with

Your dentist will check that veneers are the right option for your teeth and explain any alternatives.

  • Gaps between teeth
  • Slightly crooked teeth
  • Discolouration and staining
  • Short teeth
  • Worn edges

Two appointments

  1. Preparation

    Your teeth are prepared and impressions are taken. You leave with temporary veneers while your permanent ones are made.

  2. Fitting

    Your permanent veneers are bonded in place, and your dentist makes any adjustments so they look and feel right.
