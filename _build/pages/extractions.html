Extractions, taken gently

Having a tooth out is a common source of worry. Our aim is simple: a comfortable procedure, and a patient who feels calm and listened to throughout.

{{img:extractions|frame--corner}}

How we help you stay relaxed

  • We talk through your concerns before anything starts, and answer every question.
  • Your dentist explains what you’ll feel at each stage, so there are no surprises.
  • We discuss steps that might make the day easier for you.
  • You can choose music or a movie to focus on during treatment.
  • You’ll go home with clear aftercare instructions.

If an extraction is being considered, ask your dentist whether there are other ways to keep the tooth. Sometimes root canal therapy is an option.

Worried about how it’s healing?

Call us if you have bleeding that won’t settle, swelling that is getting worse, or pain that is increasing a few days afterwards.
