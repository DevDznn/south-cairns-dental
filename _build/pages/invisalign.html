Straighter teeth, without wires

Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable trays. Each set moves your teeth a little, until they reach their planned position.

Why people choose clear aligners

  • Discreet. The trays are clear, so most people won’t notice them.
  • Removable. Take them out to eat, brush and floss.
  • No metal. No brackets or wires.

What to know before you start

Suitability is assessed first
A trained dentist checks whether aligners can achieve what you need. Some people are better suited to braces or an orthodontist, and we’ll tell you if that’s the case.
Often 12 to 18 months
Many cases take around 12 to 18 months. Some minor cases are shorter. Your plan will give you an estimate.
Retainers afterwards
Teeth tend to drift back, so you’ll need a retainer to hold the result.
