Mouthguards made for your mouth

A mouthguard is worn over the upper teeth during contact sport. One made from impressions of your own teeth fits closely, stays put and is easier to talk and breathe in.

What a well-fitted mouthguard helps protect against

No mouthguard prevents every injury, but a good one reduces the risk.

  • Chipped, broken or knocked-out teeth
  • Jaw fractures
  • Damage to the jaw joints
  • Cuts to the lips and cheeks
  • Reduced risk of concussion

Book before the season starts

A custom mouthguard needs an appointment to take impressions and time for it to be made. Children’s mouths change as they grow, so ask your dentist when theirs should be replaced.
