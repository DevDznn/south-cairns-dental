What happens at your first visit

For a first visit we recommend a thorough consultation and dental clean, so your dentist can see the whole picture. We take sufficient time to:

More about check-ups

  1. Examine your teeth and gums

    A careful look at every tooth, your gums and the soft tissues of your mouth.

  2. Assess your jaw joints

    How your jaw moves, and any signs of strain or grinding.

  3. Take any necessary X-rays

    Close-up and full-face X-rays where needed, to see what can’t be seen by eye.

  4. Photograph anything to watch

    So you can see potential problems for yourself.

  5. Scale, clean, polish and fluoride

    Plaque and tartar are removed, then a topical fluoride treatment is applied.

  6. A printed treatment plan

    If anything needs attention, you take home the most accurate estimate we can give of the time and cost involved.

Talking through your options

Your dentist explains what they find, shows you photos where helpful, and talks you through the treatment options, including the choice to wait or do nothing. You never have to decide in the chair. Take your plan home and think it over.

Costs, explained before treatment

Your printed treatment plan sets out the estimated time and cost of any recommended treatment. Ask us about anything you’re unsure of before you go ahead.

If you have private health insurance, your fund decides what it pays back, so check your cover with them. Ask reception about current payment plans. Costs and payment FAQs

Questions or concerns

Ask as many questions as you like, before, during or after your appointment. If you’d like a break during treatment, just let your dentist know. You can also call or email reception at any time. Contact us

What you may like to bring

Reception will let you know if anything else is needed.

  • Your health fund card, if you have private health insurance
  • A list of the medicines you take, including any blood thinners
  • Details of any medical conditions or allergies
  • Recent dental X-rays or records from another dentist, if you have them
  • Your Medicare card for children who may be eligible for government dental benefits
  • A list of questions or concerns you’d like to discuss

Do you see children?

Yes. We see patients of all ages and offer free kids dentistry for eligible children. Children’s dentistry

Will I see the same dentist every time?

Yes. At South Cairns Dental you always see the same dentist, one who gets to know you and your dental history. Meet our dentists

Can I book outside normal hours?

After-hours and weekend appointments are available on request. Ask reception when you call.

I haven’t been to a dentist in years. Is that OK?

Absolutely. Many of our patients say they wish they had come to see us earlier. We’ll start with a check-up and work out a plan together.
