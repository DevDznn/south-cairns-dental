Professional teeth whitening

Professional whitening is a safe and effective way to brighten your smile. A specially designed whitening gel is applied to your teeth, where it absorbs into the enamel and lifts discolouration that has built up over time.

Your questions, answered

Every mouth is different. Your dentist will give advice for yours at your appointment.

Does whitening damage your teeth?

When you use a whitening product recommended and supervised by your dental professional, whitening is considered safe and doesn’t cause permanent damage to your teeth. Some systems sold online contain very high concentrations of bleaching agent that can damage teeth, and your dentist won’t recommend those. Your dentist also checks your teeth and gums first, so problems like decay or gum disease are dealt with before you start.

Will my teeth be sensitive?

Some patients notice mild sensitivity during whitening. It is temporary and returns to your normal level once you stop. If whitening trays are overfilled, the gums can briefly feel a slight burning and look white; this also settles quickly once the gel is rinsed away. Tell your dentist if your teeth are already sensitive.

How often can I whiten my teeth?

It varies from person to person, and depends a lot on diet and lifestyle. If you drink a lot of tea, coffee or red wine, or smoke, you’ll generally need to whiten more often. On average, a touch-up may be needed about every 6 months. Your dentist can suggest a schedule for you.

I have fillings or crowns on my front teeth. Can I still whiten?

Whitening works on natural tooth enamel. It doesn’t change the colour of fillings, crowns or veneers. Your dentist will discuss how this affects your result, and whether those restorations may need updating afterwards to match.
