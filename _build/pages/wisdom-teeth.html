Wisdom teeth

Wisdom teeth don’t always cause trouble, and they don’t always need to come out. A proper assessment tells you which situation you’re in.

{{book_buttons}}

Signs a wisdom tooth may be causing problems

If you notice any of these, book an assessment.

  • Pain at the back of the mouth
  • Swelling of the gum or jaw
  • A bad taste or bad breath
  • Infection around the tooth
  • Crowding of nearby teeth
  • Gum or tooth problems behind the wisdom tooth
{{img:wisdom|frame--corner-bl}}

Assessment comes first

Your dentist will examine your mouth and take an X-ray to see how the wisdom teeth are sitting, including any that haven’t come through.

From there, you’ll get a clear recommendation. If removal is advised, your dentist will explain the procedure, the costs and what recovery usually involves before you decide.

Swelling that spreads to your eye or neck, or makes it hard to swallow or breathe, needs urgent medical attention. See our emergency page.
