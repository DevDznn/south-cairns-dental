Practice philosophy

More than just a job to us

We take great pride in providing the very best dental treatment, at competitive prices, in the most relaxing environment we can create. Our team takes a genuine interest in your dentistry.

We take the time to listen and understand what you want to achieve, then help guide you down the right path to get there. Whatever you decide, we respect your choices.

  • Do you want a beautiful smile?
  • Do you want to be out of pain?
  • Do you want to replace a missing tooth or teeth?

Has it been a while? Feeling anxious? That’s OK.

We help many anxious patients overcome their worries and complete their treatment. Many of our patients tell us they wish they’d come to see us sooner.

Your first visitPatient FAQs

The same dentist every time

You’ll always see the same dentist, one who has taken the time to get to know you and your dental history.

Calm and in control

Our aim is to make every visit as comfortable as possible, while keeping you calm and in control.

Music or a movie

Let us know what music we can play for you, or choose a movie to watch while your treatment is completed.

Affordable care

Fees are explained before treatment begins. Ask us about payment options that suit your budget.

Finding us

The yellow building on Stokes Street

We’re in the yellow building next door to Edmonton Family Medical Centre, with plenty of free onsite parking. Piccones IGA and other health and retail outlets are a short walk away.
