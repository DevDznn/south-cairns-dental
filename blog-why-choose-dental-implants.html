Why choose dental implants?

Dental implants are a proven way to replace one or more missing teeth. Here are the main advantages, and how to find out whether an implant suits you.

Dental implants are a simple and proven method to replace one or more missing teeth. A titanium post is placed in the jawbone, and a tooth (crown) is then fixed on top.

What are the advantages of dental implants?

  • Fully fixed in place, so it can’t fall out like a denture
  • Looks like a natural tooth
  • Functions and chews like a natural tooth
  • Helps stop neighbouring teeth drifting into the gap
  • Helps prevent the jawbone shrinking away where the tooth was removed

Is an implant right for me?

Implants aren’t the right choice for everyone, and they’re not the only way to replace a tooth. A bridge or a denture may suit you better. At a consultation your dentist will look at your teeth, gums and jawbone, explain the options and talk you through the costs before anything goes ahead. Read more about dental implants.
