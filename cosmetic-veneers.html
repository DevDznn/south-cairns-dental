Changing your smile

Cosmetic veneers

A veneer is a thin porcelain shell bonded to the front of a tooth. Veneers can change the shape, colour and length of teeth for a more even smile.

Things to know before choosing veneers

To fit a veneer, a thin layer of enamel is usually removed from the front of the tooth so the veneer sits naturally. This step is permanent, so veneers are a long-term commitment.

Your dentist will check the health of your teeth and gums, look at your bite, and talk about the look you want, including the colour and shape of the veneers.

What veneers can help with

Your dentist will check that veneers are the right option for your teeth and explain any alternatives.

  • Gaps between teeth
  • Slightly crooked teeth
  • Discolouration and staining
  • Short teeth
  • Worn edges

Two appointments

  1. Preparation

    Impressions are taken and your teeth are prepared, which may mean removing a small amount of enamel; sometimes none is needed. You leave with temporary veneers while your permanent ones are made.

  2. Fitting

    Your permanent veneers are bonded in place, and your dentist makes any adjustments so they look and feel right, then shows you how to care for them.

Benefits and things to consider

Your dentist will explain the benefits, risks and alternatives that apply to you.

Possible benefits

  • Can improve colour, shape and the appearance of gaps
  • Porcelain resists staining well

Things to consider

  • Removing enamel can’t be reversed
  • Veneers can chip and may need replacing in future
  • Grinding your teeth can shorten their life, and a night guard may be recommended

Alternatives to ask about

  • Teeth whitening
  • Invisalign for crooked teeth
  • Crowns for heavily damaged teeth

Looking after your veneers

Always follow the instructions your dentist gives you for your own treatment.

  • Daily careBrush and clean between your teeth as normal. Veneers need the same care as natural teeth.
  • AvoidBiting very hard things such as ice, pens or fingernails.
  • ReviewsRegular check-ups let your dentist check the veneers and the teeth underneath.

Questions about veneers

How long do veneers last?

It varies with your bite, habits and oral health. Your dentist can talk you through what to expect.

Will veneers look fake?

Your dentist will plan the shape and shade with you so they suit your face and the colour of your other teeth.

Cost and payment

The cost depends on what your mouth needs, so it can only be worked out after an examination. Fees are always explained before treatment begins.
