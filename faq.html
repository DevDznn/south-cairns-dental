First visits and new patients

What happens at a first appointment?

For new patients we recommend a thorough consultation and dental clean. Your dentist examines your teeth and gums, assesses your jaw joints, takes any necessary X-rays and photos, then scales, cleans and polishes your teeth and applies topical fluoride. If anything needs attention you take home a printed treatment plan. New patients

Is there a new patient offer?

Yes. Our new patient offer includes a comprehensive check-up, all necessary X-rays, a scale, clean and polish, fluoride treatment and a treatment plan. It is gap free with any health fund, or $199 if you’re not in a health fund. Terms and conditions apply, so please confirm the offer when you book. Special offers

What should I bring?

We suggest your health fund card if you have private health insurance, a list of the medicines you take, and details of any medical conditions. Reception will let you know if anything else is needed.

I’m nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Yes. We help many anxious patients overcome their worries. Tell reception when you book. Your dentist will explain each step, and you’re welcome to choose music or a movie during treatment.

It’s been years since I saw a dentist. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Many of our patients tell us they wish they had come in sooner. We’ll start with a check-up and work out a plan together.

General dental care

How often should I have a check-up?

It depends on your teeth, gums and risk of decay, so your dentist will recommend an interval that suits you. Regular check-ups let us find early signs of problems before they progress.

How should I look after my teeth between visits?

brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth every day, spit out the toothpaste rather than rinsing, and limit sugary food and drinks. Sources: Australian Dental Association, healthdirect.

Why do my gums bleed when I brush?

bleeding gums can be an early sign of gum disease, though there can be other causes. Keep brushing gently, and book a check-up so your dentist can find the cause. Source: healthdirect.

What does a scale and clean do?

It removes plaque and hardened tartar that brushing and flossing can’t shift, from your teeth and around your gums, then polishes the teeth smooth. Check-ups and cleans

Children’s dentistry

When should my child first see a dentist?

Australian advice is to book a first visit when the first teeth come through, or by around 12 months of age. Fun, positive early visits help children feel comfortable at the dentist. Source: Australian Dental Association.

Which toothpaste should my child use?

from the first tooth to 18 months, clean teeth with a soft brush and water unless your dental professional advises otherwise. From 18 months to 6 years, use a pea-sized amount of low-fluoride children’s toothpaste. From 6 years, use a pea-sized amount of standard fluoride toothpaste. Source: Australian Dental Association.

Do you offer free dentistry for children?

We offer free kids dentistry for eligible children. Eligibility conditions apply, so call reception and we’ll check before the visit. Children’s dentistry

What is the Child Dental Benefits Schedule?

the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS) is an Australian Government program that helps eligible families with the cost of some basic dental services for children. Eligibility depends on age and on whether your family receives certain government payments, and a benefit cap applies. Check your eligibility with Services Australia, and ask reception how it works at our practice.

Cosmetic and restorative treatments

Does professional whitening damage teeth?

When you use a whitening product recommended and supervised by your dental professional, whitening is considered safe and doesn’t cause permanent damage to your teeth. Some products sold online contain very high concentrations of bleaching agent that can harm teeth, which is why we don’t recommend them. Teeth whitening

Will whitening work on my fillings or crowns?

No. Fillings and crowns don’t whiten like natural teeth, so the result can look patchy if you have them on your front teeth. Replacing them to match, or veneers, may be options. Your dentist will check before you start.

How long does Invisalign take?

It depends on your teeth. Some minor cases take as little as 7 weeks, but around 12 to 18 months is typical. You’ll need to wear retainers afterwards to keep your result. Invisalign

How long until an implant tooth is fitted?

The permanent tooth is usually fitted after the implant has healed and settled, typically about 3 months after it is placed. Your dentist will give you a timeline at your consultation. Dental implants

How many appointments do veneers take?

Usually two. At the first, your teeth are prepared and impressions taken, and temporary veneers are fitted. At the second, your permanent veneers are bonded in place. Cosmetic veneers

Can I talk about a treatment before deciding?

Yes. Your dentist will explain whether a treatment suits you, the alternatives and the likely costs. You never have to decide on the spot. Services overview

Dental emergencies

What counts as a dental emergency?

A painful tooth or gums, broken teeth or fillings, facial swelling, trauma to your face, teeth or gums, and a broken or lost denture. Emergency dental care

Can I be seen today?

We reserve appointments every day for dental emergencies and always try to see you as soon as possible, but we can’t guarantee a same-day time. Call as early in the day as you can.

When should I go to hospital instead?

call 000 or go to a hospital emergency department if there is difficulty breathing or swallowing, swelling spreading to the eye or neck, bleeding that won’t stop, or a head injury. For health advice any time, Queensland’s 13 HEALTH line is on 13 43 25 84.

Payment options and costs

How much will my treatment cost?

That depends on what your mouth needs. After your consultation, your printed treatment plan gives the most accurate estimate we can of the time and cost involved, before any treatment begins.

Do you offer payment plans?

We can arrange interest-free payment plans through Zip for dental treatment. Ask reception about the options and terms currently available.

Can I claim on my private health insurance?

If you have extras cover, your fund may contribute towards some treatment. What you get back depends on your policy, waiting periods and limits, so check with your fund. Bring your card to your appointment.

Is there a fee for an implant consultation?

We offer a complimentary implant consultation to check whether implants suit you and to discuss your plan and costs. Before treatment goes ahead, a mould of your teeth and a special 3D X-ray are needed, so ask reception about any costs for these.

Does Medicare cover dental care?

Medicare doesn’t cover most dental services. Eligible children may be covered for some basic services under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule. Some Queenslanders with concession cards may be eligible for public dental services through Queensland Health.

Health information on this page draws on guidance from the Australian Dental Association, healthdirect, Services Australia and Queensland Health. Your dentist can advise on your own situation.
