How fees work

Every mouth is different, so we explain the costs of any recommended treatment before it begins.

  • Costs explained first

    After your consultation, your printed treatment plan sets out what is recommended and the most accurate estimate we can give of the time and cost involved, before any treatment begins.

  • Costs that depend on you

    The cost of treatment depends on what your mouth needs, so it can only be worked out after an examination. Fees are always explained before treatment begins.

  • No need to decide on the spot

    Ask reception or your dentist about anything you’re unsure of before you go ahead. You never have to decide on the spot.

Payment plans

Health funds and children’s dental benefits

Private health insurance

If you have extras cover, your fund may contribute towards some treatment. What you get back depends on your policy, so check your cover with your fund. Bring your health fund card to your appointment.

Our new patient check-up is gap free for patients with eligible private health insurance and dental cover.

New patient offer

Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS)

We accept eligible patients under the Australian Government’s Child Dental Benefits Schedule. It helps eligible families with the cost of some basic dental services for children. Eligibility depends on age and on whether your family receives certain government payments, and a benefit cap applies.

Check your eligibility with Services Australia, and bring your child’s Medicare card.

Implant and cosmetic treatment costs

These treatments are planned around your own teeth, so we don’t quote a single figure before seeing you. Costs are discussed after your consultation and given to you in writing before anything starts.

  • Dental implants

    Implant costs vary with the number of implants, the type of crown and any extra procedures. Your dentist will give you a written plan with costs after your consultation.

    Dental implants

  • Cosmetic treatment

    Costs depend on the treatment and the number of teeth. You receive the cost in writing after your consultation, before anything starts. Health funds generally don’t contribute to purely cosmetic treatment.

Questions about payment

Can’t see your question? Call reception on (07) 4055 4214.

How much will my treatment cost?

That depends on what your mouth needs. After your consultation, your printed treatment plan gives the most accurate estimate we can of the time and cost involved, before any treatment begins.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Payment plans are available through Afterpay, Zip, Fund My Dental and Denticare. Each provider has its own terms and approval process, so ask reception which option suits your treatment.

Is the new patient offer gap free with my health fund?

The new patient check-up is gap free for patients with eligible private health insurance and dental cover. Check your cover with your fund, and confirm the offer when you book.

Does Medicare cover dental care?

Medicare doesn’t cover most dental services. Eligible children may be covered for some basic services under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule, and we accept eligible patients under the CDBS. Some Queenslanders with concession cards may be eligible for public dental services through Queensland Health.
