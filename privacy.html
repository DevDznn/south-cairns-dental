Privacy policy

Your health information is personal. This page explains how South Cairns Dental handles it.

What we collect

To provide safe dental care we collect information such as your name, contact details, date of birth, medical and dental history, medicines, health fund details, and records of your treatment, including X-rays and photographs.

Why we collect it

We use your information to provide and plan your care, communicate with you about appointments, process payments and health fund claims, and meet our legal and professional obligations.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when it’s needed for your care or allowed by law. For example, we may share it with another health practitioner you’re referred to, a dental laboratory making your crown or denture, or your health fund when processing a claim. We don’t sell personal information.

How we store it

Records are kept securely, and access is limited to people who need it to do their work.

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask to see your records or to correct information that is wrong. Contact reception on (07) 4055 4214 or reception@southcairnsdental.com.au.

This website

This website doesn’t use analytics or advertising cookies. The contact form doesn’t send or store anything itself; it opens your own email app. The map on some pages is provided by Google, which may set its own cookies under Google’s privacy policy.

Questions and complaints

If you have a concern about how we’ve handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you’re not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
