Care for every stage, under one roof

From a child’s first check-up to replacing a missing tooth, our dentists provide general, restorative and cosmetic treatment at our Edmonton practice.

Everyday care

Regular visits that keep teeth and gums healthy and catch problems while they’re small.

  • Check-ups and cleansAn examination of your teeth, gums and jaw, X-rays where needed, then a scale, clean and polish. You leave with a printed plan if anything needs attention.
  • Children’s dentistryRelaxed, positive visits that help children feel at home at the dentist, with problems found early. Free dentistry for eligible children.
  • FillingsEarly decay is removed and the tooth restored with white, tooth-coloured filling material, usually in a single visit.
  • MouthguardsCustom-fitted mouthguards made from impressions of your teeth, for a snug, comfortable fit during contact sport.

Repairing teeth

Treatment for teeth that are decayed, cracked, infected or causing pain.

  • Crowns and bridgesA crown reinforces a cracked or heavily filled tooth. A bridge fills a gap using the teeth on either side.
  • Root canal therapyWhen a tooth's nerve is badly inflamed or has died, root canal treatment can often save the tooth instead of removing it.
  • ExtractionsWhen a tooth can't be saved, we explain why, talk through your worries and take it slowly so you stay comfortable.
  • Wisdom teethA consultation and X-ray show whether your wisdom teeth are a problem. Removal is only recommended when it's needed.

Replacing teeth

Options for one missing tooth, several, or a full arch.

  • Dental implantsA titanium post replaces the root of a missing tooth and supports a natural-looking crown fixed in place.
  • DenturesFirst dentures, replacements for loose ones, or implant-retained dentures, planned around comfort and fit.

Changing your smile

Whiter, straighter or more even teeth, planned with your dentist.

  • Teeth whiteningProfessional whitening supervised by your dentist, starting with a check so you know what result to expect.
  • Cosmetic veneersThin porcelain shells bonded to the front of teeth to improve gaps, stains, chips and worn or short edges.
  • Clear alignersClear, removable aligners that gradually straighten teeth, without brackets or wires. Suitability is assessed first.
