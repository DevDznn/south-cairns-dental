Meet our dentists

At South Cairns Dental you see the same dentist from one visit to the next: someone who knows your history and what matters to you. Here are the people you’ll meet.

Principal Dentist

Dr Alex Bornsztejn

Bachelor of Health Science in Dentistry, Master of Dentistry (BHSc(Dent), MDent)

  • General dentistry
  • Dental implants
  • Wisdom teeth surgery

Alex wanted to be a dentist from early childhood. He grew up in Victoria, earned his dental degree from La Trobe University and completed his Masters in rural Victoria before moving to Queensland.

He has worked across Queensland, from Brisbane to Townsville, and is now proud to call Cairns home.

Alex enjoys all aspects of general dentistry and puts a high value on continuing education, regularly attending courses run by leaders in the field. His training has taken him overseas to learn techniques in dental implants and wisdom teeth surgery.

Outside dentistry, Alex loves time on the water fishing and with friends.

Dentist

Dr Louis Fernandez (known as Louie)

Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD)

  • General dentistry
  • Oral surgery
  • Wisdom teeth
  • Dental implants

Louis, known as Louie to colleagues and patients, grew up in a family of dentists who were passionate about oral health and patient care. That upbringing shapes how he practises today, with a strong focus on clinical excellence, patient comfort and mutual understanding between dentist and patient.

He has taught at university and worked in high-intensity hospital settings managing complex medical and surgical cases. Alongside his surgical interests, he enjoys all aspects of general dentistry and helping patients reach their smile goals, and takes pride in clear explanations and finding solutions that suit each patient’s needs and priorities.

Louie values learning and has pursued educational opportunities around the world to bring new techniques and technology into his practice.

“I’ve seen a need for excellent, thoughtful and careful oral surgery in Cairns for those who find sedation either expensive or daunting. And I am excited to go on these journeys with my patients, be it wisdom teeth extractions or dental implants.”

Dr Louis Fernandez
